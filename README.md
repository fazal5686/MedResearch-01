\# MedResearch-01



\## Research Project



\### Title



Predicting 30-Day Hospital Readmission Using Patient and Hospital Characteristics



\### Research Question



Which patient and hospitalization characteristics are associated with 30-day hospital readmission?



\### Primary Outcome



30-day hospital readmission: Yes / No



\### Initial Hypothesis



Patients with previous hospital admissions and longer hospital stays may have a higher probability of 30-day readmission.
